A make.fun micro bounty entry. `main.ts` writes a single line — the funniest
excuse a dog gives in its own voice after being caught eating the homework —
to `/output/entry.txt`.

The excuse: the dog wasn't being naughty at all. It was saving its owner —
the homework was clearly a burglar in a paper disguise, and the only way to
stop it was to eat it before it stole the house. A dog who believes it did
nothing wrong.
